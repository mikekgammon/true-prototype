Investigation prompts

The “why” layer you can bring into TrueDI later. Keeps this prototype lightweight but directionally coherent.
Why is the user copying the curve daily? Hypothesis
  • They’re tracking a trigger threshold for the next tranche.
  • They’re updating an internal budget / forecast workbook.
  • They don’t have an export + delta view in-product.
Why is the MtM user checking daily? Persona
  • Finance reassurance / board pack narrative (“in/out of the money”).
  • Validation of timing decision (psychology + accountability).
  • Monitoring open exposure impacts on valuation.
What unlocks modelling? Foundations
  • Trusted consumption baseline (12–24 months).
  • Validated cost stack (bill validation confidence).
  • Contracts + tolerance band metadata.

This prototype is intentionally “thin”: it shows how a single orchestration layer can join the dots between Trading, Portfolio, and Bill Validation without creating a new module.
